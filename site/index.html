Overview

Private compliance for agent payments.

Husk is a privacy-preserving compliance layer for AI agent-to-agent payments. It proves that a payment passed sanctions and PEP screening without revealing the underlying transaction.

The public proof shows that compliance checks were satisfied. The payment amount, counterparties, and transaction details remain private. Built on Midnight, Husk makes compliance and privacy compatible by design.
